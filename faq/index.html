Frequently asked questions

AC Chargers

Are REACH EV AC chargers compatible with all-electric vehicles?

Are REACH EV AC chargers compatible with all-electric vehicles? REACH EV chargers use standard charging connectors that are compatible with most electric vehicles on the market. Please check the compatibility of your EV model with our chargers before purchasing.

Is AC charging better for the battery?

Is AC charging better for the battery? AC chargers use lower power than DC fast charging, reducing stress and slowing down wear for a long battery life. This gentle approach is perfect for daily at-home charging, keeping your battery healthy for years to come. While DC fast charging makes a negligible difference, prioritize AC charging for everyday needs to maximize battery life.

How long does it take to charge an EV using an AC charger?

How long does it take to charge an EV using an AC charger? The charging time depends on the battery capacity of the vehicle and the power rating of the charger. Generally, a full charge using an AC charger can take anywhere from 3-4 hours to overnight. We offer different AC chargers ranging from 3.3 kW to 22 kW, to meet your daily needs.

Are these chargers water-resistant?

Are these chargers water-resistant? Yes, these chargers are rated IP65, indicating they are water-resistant. While they are designed to withstand exposure to water and dust to a certain extent, it’s important to note that they may not be completely waterproof. It’s advisable to take precautions and avoid exposing the chargers to excessive water or moisture to ensure their longevity and proper functionality.

What is a DC charger for EVs and how does it differ from AC chargers?

What is a DC charger for EVs and how does it differ from AC chargers? A DC charger, short for Direct Current charger, is a type of charging station specifically designed to rapidly charge electric vehicles. Unlike AC chargers, which convert alternating current from the grid to charge the vehicle’s battery, DC chargers supply direct current directly to the battery, allowing for faster charging times.

How long does it take to charge an EV using a DC fast charger?

How long does it take to charge an EV using a DC fast charger? Charging time using a DC fast charger is significantly shorter compared to a regular AC charger. While AC chargers may take several hours to fully charge an EV, DC fast chargers can provide a substantial charge in as little as 30 minutes, depending on the vehicle and charger specifications.

Is DC fast charging bad for the battery?

Is DC fast charging bad for the battery? DC fast charging is often a concern for EV owners regarding potential battery damage. To solve this, modern EV batteries are designed to withstand fast charging without significant long-term harm. While fast charging can generate more heat and stress on the battery compared to slower charging methods, the damage to the battery is minimal. Additionally, staying within recommended charging limits and following manufacturer guidelines can help maintain battery health and longevity.

Portable Chargers

Where can I purchase a portable charger for my EV in India?

Where can I purchase a portable charger for my EV in India? REACH EV offers a wide range of portable chargers for EVs, from 3.3 kW to 22 kW. It’s essential to choose a charger that’s compatible with your EV’s charging requirements and meets safety standards and certifications.

What is a portable charger for EVs?

What is a portable charger for EVs? A portable charger for EVs is a compact device that allows you to charge your electric vehicle from standard power outlets. It’s designed to be carried easily and used wherever there’s access to electricity.

What types of electric vehicles are compatible with portable chargers in India?

What types of electric vehicles are compatible with portable chargers in India? Portable chargers are usually compatible with a wide range of electric vehicles, including cars, scooters, and bikes, that support standard charging connectors like Type 2, CCS, or CHAdeMO.

Are portable chargers safe to use with EVs?

Are portable chargers safe to use with EVs? Portable chargers are generally safe to use when operated according to the manufacturer’s instructions. However, it’s essential to follow safety guidelines, such as using certified chargers, avoiding exposure to water or extreme temperatures, and regularly inspecting the charger for any signs of damage.
