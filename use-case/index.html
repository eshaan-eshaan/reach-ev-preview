Where REACH fits

Six kinds of site, each with its own charging pattern.

EV Fleets

REACH caters to the diverse charging needs of electric fleets, whether light, medium or heavy-duty. We work closely with you to tailor solutions that optimise operational efficiency and keep your electrification strategy adaptable as your fleet expands.

A good starting point: Depots with overnight parking suit AC; quick turnaround between shifts calls for DC.

Residential Buildings

REACH chargers integrate seamlessly into residential buildings, giving residents convenient access and efficient power management.

A good starting point: Long overnight stays make AC the natural choice.

Commercial Buildings

Charging brings increased footfall, enhanced property value, additional revenue, sustainability credentials, a competitive advantage and future-proofing against changing transport trends.

A good starting point: Match charger power to how long visitors stay.

Petrol Pumps

Take the first-mover advantage: attract new EV drivers to your pump, boost revenue with convenient charging and gain an edge in the growing EV market.

A good starting point: Drivers want quick turnaround, so DC fast charging fits best.

Offices and Workplaces

Workplace charging makes driving electric more convenient and affordable for employees, and shows a commitment to advanced vehicle technology.

Parking Lots and Garages

Become a one-place solution for your customers and earn their loyalty. A charger in your parking lot generates revenue and makes your existing asset work harder.
