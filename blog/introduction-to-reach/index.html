Introduction to REACH (Reliable Electric Automotive Charging Hub)

31 October 2024

REACH’s role in India’s Mobility Revolution

India’s electric mobility landscape is rapidly evolving, driven by government initiatives like the recently announced PM-EDRIVE scheme. This ambitious program aims to boost the adoption of electric vehicles across the country.

To support this growing EV ecosystem, REACH (Reliable Electric Automotive Charging Hub), a brand under Tech Green Innovations, offers comprehensive End-to-End EV Charging solutions. From manufacturing high-quality EV chargers and components to providing expert installation and maintenance services, we’ve got you covered.

Our Range of EV Charging Solutions:

Whether you’re an individual seeking a convenient home charging solution or a business owner looking to establish a public charging station, REACH has the perfect product for you. Our diverse range of EV chargers includes:

  • Standalone EV chargers for e-2W/3Ws: Designed for durability and efficiency, the REACH EV Charger is the perfect solution to powering your electric two and three-wheelers. This versatile charger handles lead-acid, LiFePO4, and Li-ion batteries, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of vehicles. Built to withstand harsh environments, it offers reliable charging performance, making it an essential companion for your daily commute or last-mile transportation.
  • Smart Home Chargers: REACH offers a wide range of portable EV chargers and AC Chargers so you can charge up your EV from the convenance of your home. These user-friendly chargers can be controlled remotely via a mobile app, ensuring a seamless charging experience.
  • For Public Charging Stations: Our range of DC Fast Chargers caters to different needs, from e-cars to e-cargo vehicles, ideal for commercial establishments, workplaces, and public spaces, providing quick and efficient charging for a variety of EV models.

Beyond Charging Solutions

REACH goes beyond providing hardware. We offer comprehensive support to help you set up your EV charging station, whether it’s a home charger or a public charging station. Our team of experts assists you with site surveys and preparation, installation of the charger, and ongoing maintenance, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free experience in your EV charging journey. Our end-to-end EV Charging solutions comprise of:

Site Preparation: We meticulously prepare your charging station site, ensuring optimal functionality and aesthetics, including canopy manufacturing and installation, base construction, and strategic-lights placement to enhance user experience.

EV Chargers: We’re dedicated to powering India’s electric journey. Our range of EV chargers, from home-friendly AC chargers to rapid DC fast chargers, caters to diverse charging needs. All our products are designed and manufactured in India, supporting the ‘Make in India’ initiative.

Charging Management System (CMS): Our intuitive CMS allows you to remotely monitor and manage your charging stations. From tracking energy consumption, and user activity to receiving real-time alerts for any potential issues, ensuring smooth operation.

Maintenance and Support: Our dedicated team is currently present in 25+ states across India to provide ongoing maintenance and technical support to ensure your charging stations operates without failure. You can rely on us for prompt assistance and expert advice whenever needed.

Want expert consultation for your EV charging needs?

Contact us today to get personalized consultation for your specific project or to explore investment opportunities in the EV charging ecosystem. We’re excited to connect with individuals and organizations who share our passion for a sustainable future. Let’s work together to power the electric revolution of India
