Nayana is more than just an instrument cluster; it’s your electric bike’s intelligent command centre. This user-friendly dashboard, powered by a Renesas microcontroller and featuring a clear 4.3” display, provides essential vehicle information at a glance. Monitor, control, and optimise your ride with Nayana. From riding modes and battery status to motor speed, RPM, range, and trip details, every aspect of your journey is at your fingertips. Stay informed with real-time data and timely warnings, ensuring a safe and enjoyable riding experience.

  • LCD display with intuitive GUI
  • Powered by +12VDC (7V-16V) input.
  • Isolated CAN Communication with BMS and MCU
  • GPIOs for left and right winkers, high beam, and side stand
  • All I/O’s & CAN ports with short circuits and over-voltage protection.
  • It has a rechargeable power backup system (4 Hours) using a lithium polymer battery.
  • Tri-colour LED to display the charging status.
  • Single LED to indicate the battery faults.
  • Keypad for navigating through different screens.
  • Inbuilt RTC
  • IP67 Enclosure
