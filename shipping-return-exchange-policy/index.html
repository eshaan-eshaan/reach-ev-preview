Shipping, Return and Exchange Policy

Experience the convenience of shopping with REACH.

At REACH, we’re committed to delivering more than just products – we’re delivering reliability, satisfaction, convenience, and peace of mind. Our streamlined shipping, return, and exchange processes ensure that your journey with us is as smooth as possible.

Shipping:

  • Lightning-Fast Processing: Orders are processed and shipped within 12 hours of receipt (Monday to Friday)
  • Reliable Partnerships: We’ve partnered with top-tier courier services for timely and secure deliveries across the globe
  • Transparent Tracking:
    • Receive instant tracking updates via email and SMS
    • Monitor your shipment’s progress in real time through our website.


    Special Delivery Options:

    • Cash on Delivery (COD) Across India:
      • Simply call +91 88512 35018 or email: sales@reachev.in to place your COD order.
      • A nominal confirmation advance secures your order; balance payment is collected upon delivery.
      • Important: Confirmation advance is forfeited in case of order cancellation.


      Return Policy: Because we want you to love your purchase

      • 7-Day Return Window: Initiate returns within 7 days of receiving your order
      • Eligibility:
        • Items must be unused, in original condition, with all accessories and packaging
        • Proof of purchase is required for all returns
        • Products purchased during sales, promotions, or using discount codes
        • EV Accessories(due to safety and regulatory compliance)


        Initiating a Return:

        1. Reach Out: Contact our Dedicated Support Team at support@reachev.in or +91 88512 35018 within 7 days.
        2. Provide Details: Share your order number, reason for return, and any relevant information.
        3. Return Authorization: Receive a unique return authorization number and instructions from our team


        Return Shipping:

        • On Us: REACH covers return shipping costs for defective or incorrect items
        • Tracking is recommended  Please record shipping details to prevent transit losses


        Refunds:

        • Swift Processing: Refunds are processed within 5 working days of receiving and inspecting your return.
        • Original Payment Method: Credits are automatically applied to your initial payment method


        Exchange Policy: Find your perfect match

        • 7-Day Exchange Window: Initiate exchanges within 7 days of delivery
        • Conditions:
          • Unused products in original packaging with all accessories
          • Exchange for the same or different product(subject to availability)
          • Complimentary for defective or damaged items
          • The customer’s Responsibility for all other exchange cases


          Initiating an Exchange:

          1. Get in Touch: Contact our Support Team at support@reachev.in or +91 88512 35018
          2. Provide Order Details: Share your order number and exchange requirements.
          3. Guided Process: Our team will assist you through the exchange process.


          Your Satisfaction, Our Priority:

          • Dedicated Support: support@reachev.in | +91 88512 35018
          • Stay Connected: Follow us for updates, tips, and exclusive offers


          Thank you for choosing REACH. Your trust is our greatest achievement.
