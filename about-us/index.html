Born on World Environment Day

REACH, the Reliable Electric Automotive Charging Hub, is a brand of Tech Green Innovations. We started on 5 June 2023 to accelerate India’s move to sustainable mobility.

Our vision

A future where sustainable transportation is the norm and electric vehicles are part of daily life, with cleaner air, lower emissions and a healthier planet.

Our mission

To accelerate the transition to electric vehicles by building innovative, accessible and efficient charging units, and to give drivers a worry-free experience.

Designed, developed and manufactured in India.

Our chargers and PCBs are designed, engineered and manufactured in-house, with 18 built-in safety features and firmware that updates over the cloud.
